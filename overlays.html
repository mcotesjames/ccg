Interaction notes
  • The card above is the trigger, exactly as the leader cards on the board page are. The whole card opens the panel, and it announces itself to assistive technology as opening a dialog rather than reading as a link to another page.
  • The trigger: the editorial card, unchanged: its image zooms, its title sweeps the green underline and the link row's arrow nudges on hover, so nothing tells the reader it will behave differently until the panel arrives. Only the destination changes: a panel over this page rather than a new one.
  • Open: the panel slides in from the right edge over 0.55s on the house easing curve while a deep green scrim fades up behind it over 0.45s. Page scrolling locks, the panel starts at its top, and focus moves to the back row.
  • Layout: a wide panel against the right edge, full height and scrollable on its own. The back row and the portrait share the top row so the image aligns with the top of the panel; the name and role sit in a narrow left column with the bio beside them.
  • Back row: a grey return link with a left-pointing arrow, standing in for a close button. Hover or focus inks it deep green and nudges the arrow left over 0.35s.
  • Close: the back row, a click on the scrim, or Escape. Focus returns to the card that opened it and page scrolling unlocks.
  • While open: Tab cycles inside the panel and never reaches the page behind it.
  • Content: the panel is a single instance per page; page JS fills the name, role, portrait and bio from the chosen card, so one panel serves the whole listing.
  • Responsive: the panel runs the full width below tablet. On mobile it becomes one column, portrait, name, role, bio, with a hairline under the name and a shorter portrait.
  • With reduced motion enabled the panel and scrim appear without the slide or the fade.
02

Confirmation modal

A form reads its answers back before it sends

Interaction notes
  • Fill in or change the enquiry above, then choose Send enquiry. Instead of sending straight away, the form opens a review of everything it is about to send.
  • Open: a deep green scrim fades up over 0.45s while the dialog rises 24px into place and fades in on the house easing curve. Page scrolling locks and focus lands on the send button, so a reader who is happy can confirm with a single key press.
  • Layout: a centred white dialog, 880 wide. A green step line, the heading and a short prompt sit over a hairline; the answers below read as a ruled ledger with the label in a narrow grey column and the answer in black beside it.
  • Groups: answers fall into three groups, the enquiry, the person and the declarations, each under a green title on a green rule. Each group has its own Edit route on the right.
  • Edit: closes the dialog and puts the cursor straight into the first field of that group, so a typo in an email address is one step from fixed.
  • Long answers: the message keeps its line breaks. When the ledger outgrows the screen it scrolls on its own; the heading and the send row stay fixed.
  • Blank answers: read as Not provided in pale sage, so a missed optional field is visible without looking like an error.
  • Send: the button reads Sending… and holds while the request is in flight, then the dialog becomes the thank-you: the gold hearts mark, a personal thank-you and where the reply will go, rising gently into place.
  • Close: the cross (which turns a quarter and inks green on hover), Back to form, a click on the scrim, or Escape. Focus returns to the button that opened it; nothing typed into the form is lost.
  • While open: Tab cycles inside the dialog and never reaches the page behind it.
  • Responsive: on mobile the dialog takes the whole screen, sliding up from the foot, with each label stacked over its answer. The heading stays at the top and the send row at the bottom while the answers scroll between them.
  • With reduced motion enabled the dialog, scrim and thank-you appear without movement.
