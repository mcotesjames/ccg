Interaction notes
  • Count-up: the figure animates from zero to its value the first time the cell scrolls into view, once only, 2s on an expo ease-out, so it moves fast early and glides into its final value; decimal figures animate with their precision. With reduced motion enabled the final value shows immediately.
  • Anatomy: a green line-drawn icon centred on a small square cream plate, the number at the h1 scale in tabular numerals with its unit running inline beside it in green, and a one-line ink-black label held to a readable measure.
  • Framing: cells sit in a hairline grid where the outer frame and inner dividers never double up.
  • Grids: three columns as standard, shown here at the full six-cell module, two rows of three, or a centred four-up row variant; both step to two columns on tablet and pairs on mobile.
  • The number runs at the h1 scale, so it steps down with the type ramp at each breakpoint.

Interaction notes
  • No JavaScript. This is the browser's native disclosure element. Open and close, keyboard operation, and the screen-reader announcement all come from the browser, there is nothing to bind and nothing to initialise. Do not reimplement it with a click handler.
  • Open / close: the panel snaps; there is no height animation. Native disclosure cannot animate its own height, and no attempt is made to fake one. Only the toggle animates.
  • The toggle: a small hairline square carrying a plus drawn from two thin bars. Opening fills it deep green with a white plus, and the vertical bar rotates flat over 0.3s on the house easing curve, leaving a minus. The square is decorative and hidden from assistive tech, the summary already announces its own state.
  • Hover: the question moves from deep green to green over 0.25s. The whole summary row is the target, not just the words.
  • Several open at once: the native default is kept, opening one item never closes another. There is no single-open variant, so do not assume one.
  • Opening state: the first item ships open, so the block never presents as a wall of closed rows.
  • Anatomy: a hairline above every item and one below the last. The question runs at lead size with the toggle pushed to the right edge and centred against the row; the answer is grey, gently indented and held to a readable measure. On mobile that indent goes and the answer sits flush.
  • Closing row: optional, and not part of the accordion, a note left, a secondary link CTA right, wrapping when the pair will not fit. Both current uses route the reader on rather than leaving them at a dead end.
  • Keyboard: the summary is focusable by default and toggles on Enter or Space. The default marker is suppressed for WebKit, which is what lets the custom toggle stand in for it.
  • Source: a shared component. The pages supply only the section band around it, its padding and its ground, which is cream on the contact page and white on the internship page.
03

Document row

Ruled row of a dated publication · cover, year, title, description, view

2023/24 Fifth edition GRESB 5-star and Global Sector Leader recognition; advances in governance, risk management and green performance. View
Interaction notes
  • A single row from the Previous editions archive on the Sustainability Reports page. Rows stack into a ruled list for anything a reader scans by date and opens: reports, white papers, research, job openings.
  • Anatomy: a landscape cover on the left, then the year in small text, the title, and a one-line description at body size, with the view link pushed to the right. All text is black. A hairline runs above the list and beneath every row.
  • Hover: the whole row is the link. The cover zooms 4% over 0.8s and the view arrow nudges right, as on the standard link. The text does not change.
  • Without a cover: the same row can run text-only, with the year, title and description on the left and the view link on the right.
  • Responsive: on mobile the row stacks: the cover above, then the text, then the view link beneath.
04

Film plate

16:9 poster frame · gold play control

Interaction notes
  • How film sits on the site: a poster frame carrying the play control, used for the culture film on the careers page and the Hearts film on the membership page.
  • Anatomy: a 16:9 frame with the poster filling it, cropped rather than letterboxed, on a deep green ground while the poster loads. The play control is a solid gold disc with a white triangle, centred on the frame.
  • Hover / focus: the poster zooms 2% over 0.8s and the gold disc grows 8% over 0.3s. Both respond wherever the reader is on the frame, and to keyboard focus on the control.
  • Playback: the control is not yet wired to a player; it waits for the film itself. Pressing it currently does nothing.
  • Width: the page decides. The careers page frames it within the page gutters on a cream band; the membership page runs it wider.
  • Responsive: the frame keeps its 16:9 at every width and the disc steps down on mobile, where a page may also run the frame edge to edge.
