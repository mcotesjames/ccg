Assembled page modules rather than building blocks: each one is a fixed arrangement of parts with behaviour of its own, built for a single job on a single page. Take a module whole, its layout, its timings and its keyboard handling are one piece. The reusable parts they are built from live in the component library.

Interaction notes
  • Engine: this is the shared cover story component, its rotation, pausing, anatomy, responsive behaviour and build gotchas are documented there. The notes below cover only what this page adds.
  • Framing header: this page's addition, not part of the component. It sits above the stage in the module's own dark ground, carries a counting figure in warm beige, and steps the statement down one scale. The gradient is retuned to suit it: dark at the top for the statement, clear through the middle so the photograph reads, dark again at the foot for the rotating detail.
  • Compression: the page also pulls the band noticeably below the component's default floor, and steps the story title down one scale, so the header and the spotlight can share one screen.
