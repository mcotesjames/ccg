02

Status tabs

Segmented filter: the active tab fuses with the band below

Interaction notes
  • Click: the active plate flips white to deep green with lime text instantly; inactive tabs warm on hover, also instantly.
  • Anatomy: each tab pairs its label with a zero-padded count set smaller and lighter; tabs are divided by hairlines inside a hairline frame.
  • In context: the tab row sits flush against a deep green band so the active tab reads as one surface with the filtered content beneath it; switching resets dependent filters and pagination.
  • Responsive: below tablet width the row stretches full width with tabs sharing space equally; on mobile the padding tightens and the counts disappear.
03

Text filter

Lead-size filter tabs: gold underline marks the active choice

Small variant: compact facet for control rows

Interaction notes
  • Click: the chosen item darkens to deep green and takes a 2px gold underline instantly; the previous choice releases both. Single-select.
  • Hover / focus: inactive items preview the deep green ink without the underline.
  • Anatomy: plain lead-size text resting in grey with generous gaps, no frames or plates; the gold underline is the only active mark.
  • Rule of use: the standard content filter for archives and listings: categories on the news archive, years on awards. Selecting re-filters content and resets pagination.
  • Small variant: the same behaviour at small text size with tighter gaps, for compact control rows beside a primary filter, like the availability facet on the portfolio browser.
04

Category pills

Rounded outline filter on dark grounds: active fills lime

Interaction notes
  • Click: toggles the pill on or off instantly; active pills fill lime with deep green text, and any number can be active at once.
  • Hover: inactive pills brighten; both outline and text step up towards white.
  • Anatomy: fully rounded (the only rounded element in the system), with small labels, tight gaps, wrapping to as many rows as needed.
  • Rule of use: the dark-ground category facet in a filter stack: status tabs pick the primary set and category pills refine it by any combination; changing any facet re-filters and resets pagination.

Interaction notes
  • Click trigger: the options panel slides down from behind the trigger over 0.25s on the house easing curve; the chevron flips in step and the options cascade in with a slight stagger.
  • Click option: the value swaps, the active row takes a gold rule and deep green ink, the panel closes and focus returns to the trigger.

Site navigation

Sticky reveal + mega menu: contained viewport below

Interaction notes
  • The frame above is a real viewport running the production header. Scroll inside it, and hover a nav item at the top to open the mega menu.
  • At top: the header is transparent over the hero, with white text and an inverted logo.
  • Scroll down past 92px: the header slides away upward over 0.42s on the house easing curve.
  • Scroll up: any upward scroll brings the header back as a fixed solid white bar with dark ink, the logo un-inverted and the CTA in its solid deep green form. Back at the very top it reattaches and turns transparent again.
  • Nav links: hover or focus draws a thin underline from the left over 0.3s; leaving retracts it to the right.

Mobile navigation: switch to the 414px viewport above

Interaction notes
  • The bar (mobile): drops to 72px tall and keeps three elements: logo, a slimmed Sales & Leasing plate, and the burger. Search moves into the menu panel.
  • Burger: three hairlines with the middle one shorter; hover extends it to full width. Open crosses the outer pair and retires the middle line.
  • Tap burger: a full-screen white panel slides down from the top over 0.55s; the section rows cascade in as the sheet lands, the bar fuses with it and page scrolling locks.
  • Tap a section: the five-row list sweeps left and fades as the child links cascade in from the right with a Back row and the section title. Back reverses; closing the panel resets to the top level.
  • Utility foot: a full-width Search row above Contact Us and the language toggle. The active language reads by weight, not underline.
  • Escape: closes the panel and returns focus to the burger.

Mega menu: hover a nav item in the desktop viewport

Interaction notes
  • Open (hover / focus / click a nav item): the panel fades in and drops 10px into place over roughly a third of a second. The header turns solid while open, and the open item keeps its underline.
  • Moving between items: repopulates the panel in place, with no close-and-reopen flicker.
  • Layout: four columns: overview (kicker, title, description), two numbered link lists split evenly, and a feature card with image, title and a read-the-story link. A utility row beneath carries Contact Us and the language toggle.
  • Link rows: hover or focus shifts the row slightly right, inks it green and fades in a green arrow. Numerals are set in the serif italic.
  • Feature card: the image zooms gently over 0.7s; the link underline darkens to green.
  • Close: a moment after the pointer leaves the header (brushing past never flickers it); immediately on an outside click; Escape closes and returns focus to the triggering item.
  • Below desktop width the mega menu does not exist; navigation moves to the mobile panel above.
02

Footer

Global footer: one source, identical on every page

Interaction notes
  • One shared footer, identical on every page; the sitemap is maintained in a single place.
  • Layout: six equal columns on the deep green ground: five navigation groups mirroring the main menu, plus a contact column with address, phone and email. The bottom row holds the inverted wordmark, centred legal links and the social icons.
  • Hover (social): icons lift 2px over 0.25s. Navigation links rest at a softened white.
  • Responsive: the navigation grid steps from six columns to three, then two on mobile; the bottom row stacks and centres: the wordmark grows to three-quarters of the column, the legal row tightens, and the footer gains extra bottom padding.

Carousels

Three track behaviours: free-running showcase, stepped auto-advance, and the paged card carousel.

01

Showcase carousel

Infinite auto-scroll · drag · custom cursor · detail modal

Interaction notes
  • Autoplay: the track drifts left continuously at about 54px per second, looping seamlessly and endlessly in both directions.
  • Drag: pressing pauses the drift and scrubs the track 1:1 with the pointer. Small movements still count as clicks; larger drags never open a card. The drift resumes shortly after release.
  • Hover a card: the image zooms gently over 0.8s; the deep green plate lightens instantly.
  • Custom cursor: over the images the native cursor is replaced by a red View disc that follows the pointer, scaling up with a quick fade.
  • Click a card: opens the detail modal, a full-screen dialog over a blurred deep-green scrim. The panel rises and settles over 0.55s while the hero image eases from a slight zoom to rest.
  • While open: the carousel pauses and page scrolling locks; keyboard focus stays inside the dialog.
  • Close: the cross button, a click on the backdrop, or Escape; focus returns to the card that opened it and the drift resumes.
  • Responsive: cards run 364px wide with a tall image crop on desktop, stepping down on mobile.
  • With reduced motion enabled the drift stops and the carousel becomes drag-only.

Onward card

Cross-page routing at section ends: green title sweep

Interaction notes
  • Hover / focus: the image zooms gently over 0.8s; a green underline sweeps under the title over 0.45s and the arrow nudges right.
  • Anatomy: a 4/3.4 image and a title row with an inline arrow. The whole card is a single link.

Interaction notes

01

Breadcrumb

Page trail: sits directly beneath the page hero

  1. Home
  2. Parent section
  3. Current page
Interaction notes
  • Structure: a trail of ancestor links ending in the current page, which is plain text and never a link. The interpunct separators are part of the design, not typed content.
  • Hover / focus: ancestor links darken from grey to deep green instantly; the current page sits in deep green permanently.
  • Placement: a full-width cream band directly beneath the page hero on core pages, with the standard page gutters.
  • Set small, with even breathing room around each separator.

01

Stat cell

Key figures: numbers count up on scroll

0Msq ft

One-line label describing the figure at small size

Interaction notes
  • Count-up: the figure animates from zero to its value the first time the cell scrolls into view, once only; decimal figures animate with their precision. With reduced motion enabled the final value shows immediately.
  • Anatomy: a small green line-drawn icon, the number at a large display scale in tabular numerals with its unit dropping to its own line below in green, and a one-line grey label held to a readable measure.
  • Framing: cells sit in a hairline grid where the outer frame and inner dividers never double up.
  • Grids: three columns as standard, or a centred four-up row; both step to two columns on tablet and pairs on mobile.
  • The number runs at a deliberate display scale that sits outside the standard type ramp.
