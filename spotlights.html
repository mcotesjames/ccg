Spotlights

Story modules with a numbered index: photography behind or beside the copy, never tracking sideways. Three members: the cover story rotates on its own; the spotlight split and the plate spotlight wait to be clicked.

01

Cover story

Full-bleed photography · 6s rotation, 10s once chosen · gold progress underline

Interaction notes
  • Instances: the home page's community spotlights and the social page's people spotlight are both this component, a change here reaches every one of them.
  • A page may add to it: the social page sets a framing statement above the stage on the module's own dark ground, carrying a headline figure in warm beige, and retunes the gradient to suit: dark at the top for the statement, clear through the middle so the photograph reads, dark again at the foot for the rotating detail. It also pulls the band below the default floor and steps the story title down one scale, so the header and the spotlight share one screen. Those are page decisions, not variants of the component.
  • Rotation: the module advances every 6 seconds, or every 10 once the reader has picked a story, and wraps. A gold hairline fills each index row over exactly that interval, running right to left, so the reader can see how long the current story has left.
  • Media change: the photographs are stacked and cross-faded, the outgoing one dissolving into the incoming over 0.9s. Each photograph also drifts from a 5% zoom back to rest over 6.5s, so the image is never quite still while its story is showing.
  • Detail change: the copy block is replaced, fading in and rising slightly over 0.7s. It is a live region, so a screen reader announces each new story as it arrives.
  • Click an index row: jumps to that story and restarts the timer. From then on the band keeps rotating, but at a slower ten seconds a story, so a reader who has started choosing has longer to read before it moves on. The gold line always runs the length of whichever interval is in play.
  • No pause: nothing stops the rotation. Hovering the band has no effect at all; it neither pauses nor slows.
  • Index anatomy: a right-aligned column at the foot of the stage. Each row pairs a zero-padded numeral with its label; resting rows sit at 48% white, hover lifts them to 85%, and the active row is solid white with a gold numeral and a full-width hairline beneath.
  • Stage anatomy: detail left, index right, both pinned to the bottom of the band. Two crossed gradients sit over the photograph, one vertical, one horizontal, so type stays readable wherever the image is light.
  • Defaults: a tall band floor on the deep green ground, with the component's own gradient. Pages may compress the floor or retune the gradient, those are page decisions, quoted on the instance's own entry, not variants of the component.
  • Responsive: at tablet width the stage flips to a single column with the index above the detail and both aligned left. On mobile the title and index rows each step down a size.
  • Reduced motion: the rotation never starts and the module rests on its first story. Clicking the index still works.
  • Two gotchas for the build: the component ships hidden and does nothing until a page switches it on, and it ships empty, media, index and detail are all written by the page from a story list. There is no markup contract for a story, so the list is the contract.
  • Keyboard gap: index rows are buttons, so they focus and activate, but there are no arrow keys and nothing stops the rotation, so a keyboard user tabbing the index has stories changing under them. The service tabs handle this correctly and are the pattern to copy.
02

Spotlight split

Framed media · numbered index rail · click-driven, no autoplay

Optional section header

A heading that frames the stories below.

Optional link
Interaction notes
  • Instances: the home page's community spotlights and the placemaking module on the our-approach page. Our-approach drops the numerals, a page decision, overriding the item grid to a single full-width column.
  • Versus the cover story: same family, opposite temperament. Click-driven with no autoplay and no progress line; sits on white with the photograph held in a frame beside the copy rather than behind it.
  • Click a row: the photographs cross-fade over 0.7s while the incoming image settles from a 4% zoom over 1.1s; the detail block swaps with a 0.6s rise-and-fade. Re-clicking the active row does nothing.
  • Index anatomy: baseline-aligned rows, a serif-italic numeral in its own narrow column beside the label at the h4 scale. Resting rows sit in sage; hover lifts them to ink; the active row is deep green with a green numeral.
  • Layout: a large media frame beside the rail, roughly a half-and-half split; the rail holds the index above and the detail below, pushed apart, inset from the media with the page gutter on its right. The section header above is optional and part of the markup contract.
  • Ships empty: media, index and detail are all written by page JS from a story array, the array is the contract. Unlike the cover story it is visible without an enabling rule.
  • Responsive: at tablet width the split stacks into one column, the media shortening and gaining a right gutter; at mobile the media shortens again, the gaps tighten, and the detail moves above the index, the story copy reads before the control that switches it.
  • Reduced motion: the cross-fade becomes a quick opacity swap with no zoom, and the detail swaps without animating.
  • Keyboard: rows are buttons, so they focus and activate, and with no autoplay nothing moves under focus, so the cover story's keyboard gap does not apply here.
03

Plate spotlight

Numbered index beside a media-and-copy plate · reader-driven

First service

A paragraph of body copy describing the service, sitting on the green plate beside its image. The panel runs 16:9 so the stage never changes height as the services advance.

  • An optional inclusions list, marked with lime dots
  • One line per inclusion
  • Kept short enough to hold the panel ratio
Optional panel CTA

Second service

Panels without the aside carry a title and body copy only, the setting the property services page uses. The inclusions list and the CTA are both optional, and a module may mix panels that have them with panels that do not.

Third service

A paragraph of body copy describing the service. Copy runs long enough to fill the plate but never long enough to break the 16:9 card, which is the constraint the writing has to work inside.

A fourth service with a longer label

Index labels wrap onto a second line rather than truncating, and the numbered column holds its width, so a long service name never pushes the index out of alignment.

Interaction notes
  • Reader-driven: nothing advances on its own. A story stays up until another is chosen, so the reader sets the pace.
  • Click a row: switches to that story immediately. A full gold hairline sits under the active row, marking where the reader is in the list.
  • Keyboard: one tab stop reaches the index, then the arrow keys move between stories. Up and left go back, down and right go forward, both wrapping at the ends; focus follows the selection.
  • Panel change: the outgoing panel is replaced by the incoming one, which fades and rises into place over 0.5s on the house easing curve.
  • Index anatomy: hairline-ruled rows in a column held to 350px, with the plate taking whatever width is left. Each row pairs a gold serif-italic numeral in its own narrow column with the label; resting labels are muted white, hover lifts them to full white, and the active row is lime. Long labels wrap onto a second line rather than truncating, and the numbered column holds its width.
  • Plate anatomy: a 16:9 card split in half, image left, green copy plate right, so image and plate read as one card. The fixed ratio is what stops the stage jumping as stories change. The plate holds a title, body copy and an optional aside: an inclusions list with lime dot markers, and a light secondary CTA.
  • Responsive: below 1000 the stage stacks, the plate drops its fixed ratio and the media becomes 2:1; at mobile the media is 3:2, the plate insets tighten and the index steps down one size. The numbered index stays a vertical list at every width, it never becomes a horizontal tab row.
  • Reduced motion: panels change without the fade. Clicks and arrow keys still work.
  • Optional timer: a module can ask to advance on its own at a set interval. The gold underline then fills left to right over that interval and freezes on hover, so the reader can see how long a story has left. No page uses it at present.
  • Instances: the pillars on the Our Approach page, the services on Property Services, and the venue categories on Membership Rewards are all this module. Property Services runs panels of title and body copy only; Membership Rewards adds the inclusions list and a light CTA.
  • Ground: the module sets its own colours but not its background; the section around it supplies the deep green. A light colourway swaps the deep green for white with a cream plate.
