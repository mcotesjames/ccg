01

Showcase carousel

Infinite loop · drag · arrows · custom cursor · detail modal · control bar

01 / 05
Interaction notes
  • Resting track: no auto-scroll — the track sits still until dragged or stepped, and loops seamlessly and endlessly in both directions.
  • Control bar: the standard count · progress · arrows row. The count is driver-owned on the shared logic — positions the track can rest at — and the marquee loop makes every card a resting position, so the total is the property count. The count and thumb tick live as cards pass the left edge, dragged or stepped; the thumb spans one slide (100% / count).
  • Arrows: step one card, easing the track to the next boundary. With reduced motion the step is an instant jump.
  • Drag: pressing scrubs the track 1:1 with the pointer, cancelling any arrow step in flight. Small movements still count as clicks; larger drags never open a card.
  • Hover a card: the image zooms gently over 0.8s; the green plate deepens instantly.
  • Custom cursor: over the images the native cursor is replaced by a ground-yellow View disc in deep green ink that follows the pointer, scaling up with a quick fade.
  • Click a card: opens the detail modal, a full-screen dialog over a blurred deep-green scrim. The panel rises and settles over 0.55s while the hero image eases from a slight zoom to rest.
  • While open: page scrolling locks and keyboard focus stays inside the dialog.
  • Close: the cross button, a click on the backdrop, or Escape; focus returns to the card that opened it.
  • Responsive: cards run 364px wide with a tall image crop on desktop, stepping down on mobile.
  • With reduced motion enabled the arrow step lands instantly instead of easing; drag is unaffected.

Interaction notes
  • Autoplay: steps one stat every 3 seconds; each step slides the track over 0.8s on the house easing curve. Pressing an arrow moves immediately and restarts the timer.
  • Endless: the loop continues in both directions with no visible rewind.
  • UI: the count shows the current position, zero-padded, and its total is driver-owned like the manual carousel's — positions the track can rest at. Because the seamless loop makes every card a resting position, that total equals the slide count at every width. The thumb spans one slide (100% / count) and advances by its own width per step.
  • Responsive: four stats visible on desktop, two on tablet, one on mobile; numbers sit in warm beige at a display scale, bottom-right of each cell, with hairline dividers between cells.
  • With reduced motion enabled the autoplay never starts; the arrows still work.

Interaction notes
  • Arrows / drag: one card per step over 0.7s on the house easing curve; the track drags 1:1 with grab cursors and snaps to the nearest card on release. The page still scrolls vertically over it.
  • Ends: this carousel stops at either end; it does not loop, unlike the stats carousel.
  • On move: the zero-padded count updates and the green progress thumb repositions along the hairline. The count is positions, not cards: the total is how many places the track can rest at (cards − visible + 1, recomputed on resize), so three-up with six cards reads 01 / 04 and ends on 04 / 04.
  • Cards: the editorial full card in its static setting, with the title arrow, role and spec rows dropped — image, meta row, title, excerpt and read-more remain — and the image re-cropped from the family's 4/3.4 default to 16/9 for the track.
  • Hover a card: the read-more arrow nudges right. The static setting drops the image zoom and the title sweep — inside a drag track the card presents rather than navigates.
  • Responsive: three cards across on desktop, two on tablet, one on mobile, where each card sits slightly short of the column so the next peeks in.
